- ctx=4096: BF16=448 → INT8=512 (1.14x resident)

- ctx=8192: BF16=288 → INT8=176 (0.61x resident)
  - KV block capacity: BF16=21432 → INT8=41565 (1.94x, 量化核心收益)
  - 注: INT8 ctx=8192 仅测到 176 (候选上限), 未充分扩展; 容量差异见 block 数

## E. Reuse Ratio Break-even

- V5 throughput 未超过 V1 (L1 直接命中 > L2 分层, 工作集适配 L1)
- V5 P95 TTFT < V1 at reuse_ratio=100% (V5=1693.95ms vs V1=2418.53ms)
- 洞察: L2 价值在 L1 容量不足 (工作集溢出) 时显现; 本实验 8 hot prefix 适配 L1

## F. 8192 Prefix Config Boundary

- max_blocks=512: skipped_too_large (8192+16suffix=513 blocks > 512)
- max_blocks=513: success (精确边界), TTFT 134.32ms

## G. Coalescing — H2D Reduction

- conc=64: V5 load_jobs=8 vs V4 load_jobs=24
- coalesced_requests: V5=504

## H. Quantized L2 Traffic Saving

- INT8 L2 vs BF16: -48% traffic, saved 615.5 GB total across runs

## I. Hot-path Device Sync Discipline

- max device_wide_sync_hotpath_count = 0 (§四 要求=0)
